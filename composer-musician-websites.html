What a composer site needs

  • A works catalog with every piece on its own page: instrumentation, duration, score preview, audio, program note, and where to buy or rent the score.
  • Discography with streaming and purchase links.
  • Press page with real outlet logos linking to the articles, not text wordmarks.
  • A downloadable press kit: bios at three lengths, high-resolution photos, and a booking form.
  • Events and premieres you can add yourself.

Designed for your music, not a template

Most musician templates give you a hero photo of a piano and a list of dates. What I build is decided by what you write and who needs to find it. For a composer of sacred music that means a works catalog a choir director can search by instrumentation, liturgical season, or difficulty, with a score preview and a recording on the same page, and typography and color that suit the repertoire. For a songwriter or band it means the discography up front, embedded audio or video, tour dates the venue can trust, and a press kit a promoter can download in one click. Either way the design choices, typeface, palette, imagery, come from your catalog, and the site is built so a presenter, publisher, or program director gets to the score or the recording in one or two taps.

Keeping it current

New work, new recording, new performance date: you add them yourself with the built-in editor. No developer needed, no monthly fee.
