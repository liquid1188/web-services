Websites for musicians and composers

Your website might need a place for recordings and upcoming performances, or it might need a catalog of compositions, scores, and program notes.

I build both, and I can help with press materials, booking forms, streaming links, and other information that performers, presenters, and listeners need.

What your website can include

  • Recordings and embedded music players
  • A discography
  • A searchable works catalog
  • Score previews and program notes
  • Performances and upcoming events
  • Press information
  • Booking forms
  • Links to streaming and purchasing platforms

Organizing the music

For composer Frank La Rocca, I built a catalog of 59 compositions with scores, recordings, and individual work pages.

Other musicians may need a simpler website focused on releases, performances, or booking.

I’ll help determine what belongs on the site and how to make it easy to navigate.

Adding new music

Custom Websites and Full Builds include an editor for routine updates, so you can add recordings, change dates, and update your catalog without having to contact me every time.
