A website doesn’t have to cost thousands.

Most of the websites I build cost between $500 and $1,200. That includes custom design, the pages you need, and help getting everything online.

If you only need something simple, I offer starter websites for $300 to $500. Larger projects, particularly those involving archives, online stores, or custom features, start at $1,200 and are priced by scope. Most of them land under $2,500.

I’ll give you a written estimate before any work begins.

Why I build websites this way

I generally prefer custom development because it gives me more control over the design and lets me build around what a client actually needs.

It can also make a straightforward website easier to maintain without depending on an elaborate page builder or a long list of plugins.

I still use established platforms when they’re the better fit. For an online store, for example, Shopify may make more sense than building a shopping system from scratch.

I’m interested in what works for the project, not defending one way of building websites against every other.

What happens after launch?

Before a website goes live, I check the pages, forms, links, and layout across different screen sizes.

Custom Websites and Full Builds include 30 days of post-launch support. If something isn’t working because of a problem with the build, I’ll correct it.

You can also make routine changes yourself using the editor, or ask me to handle them.

Help after launch
