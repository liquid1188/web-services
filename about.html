I’m Andrew, and you’ll work directly with me.

Lickity Split is my web design business, based in Baltimore. I work directly with clients on everything from the first conversation to the finished website, including the design, writing, and technical setup.

My background is in business, communications, and publishing. I’ve founded a nonprofit, edited books, and written for national publications. That experience shapes how I approach websites, particularly for people and organizations with a large body of work to present.

I care about how a site looks, and just as much about how the information is organized, whether the writing is clear, and how easy it is to keep up to date.

A little about my background

I studied business administration at the Community College of Baltimore County, communication studies at Towson University, and Catholic studies at Franciscan University of Steubenville.

My work has included journalism, publishing, communications, and nonprofit administration. I’ve also completed professional development programs through Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses, Johns Hopkins University, and McKinsey Forward.

I founded the Likoudis Legacy Foundation, whose website I built and continue to maintain.

These experiences have given me a useful perspective on what small organizations, writers, and independent professionals need from a website, particularly when they don’t have a technical staff or marketing department.

Based in Baltimore

I live and work in Baltimore, although many of my clients are elsewhere. Most projects can be handled remotely, and I’m happy to meet locally when it makes sense.

Common questions

Who owns the website?

You do. Once the project is complete, the website files are yours. I can continue hosting the site, or help you move it elsewhere.

Some features may depend on third-party services with their own accounts or terms. I’ll identify those before we begin.

Will my site appear in your portfolio?

Yes, unless you ask me not to. Websites I build may be shown in my portfolio, on social media, and in advertising for Lickity Split Web. That can include screenshots and recordings of the site with the name, logo, photographs, and text that appear on it.

If you would rather keep yours private, tell me before we start or at any point afterward, and I’ll leave it out of anything new.

Can I update it myself?

Yes. Custom Websites and Full Builds include an editor set up for your site, with presets for the things you add most, such as an event, a book, or a post. You fill in a short form and the site handles the layout.

You can also change text, replace photographs, and save drafts. I’ll show you how to use it before launch.

How long does a website take?

A small starter site can sometimes be ready in about 48 hours once I have the material. Custom websites usually take about a week, while larger projects take longer.

I’ll give you an expected timeline with the estimate.

What does it cost to maintain?

Hosting is currently included for the straightforward websites I build.

You’ll still need a domain name, and some projects may involve separate costs for email, e-commerce, newsletters, booking services, or other integrations.

I’ll explain any recurring costs in advance.

Can you move an existing website?

Yes. I’ve moved websites from Wix, Squarespace, and older blogging platforms, including archives with thousands of posts.

I’ll review the existing site before giving you an estimate.

What if something needs fixing?

Custom Websites and Full Builds include 30 days of post-launch support. If there’s a problem caused by my work, I’ll correct it.

Additional changes and new features can be quoted separately.

Do you use AI in your development process?

Yes. I use AI-assisted development tools to help with coding and other technical work, which allows me to complete projects more efficiently and keep my prices affordable. I remain responsible for the design, testing, and finished website.

Have a project in mind?

Tell me what you’re working on and what you need. I’ll review the details and give you an estimate.
