Author websites

An author website should make it easy to find your books, read your work, and get in touch.

I build sites for writers who need a professional home for their publications, whether they’re launching a first book or bringing years of writing together in one place.

What I can build

  • Individual book pages with covers, descriptions, and purchase links
  • Bibliographies and article archives
  • Press kits with biographies, photographs, and media information
  • Speaking and interview inquiries
  • Newsletter subscriptions
  • Blog integrations
  • An editor for routine updates

A website that fits your writing

A novelist promoting a new series needs a different website from an academic writer with decades of essays, lectures, and publications.

I can build book pages, bibliographies, press kits, speaking information, and archives, with a design suited to the author’s work.

Sometimes that means starting with a single book and adding to the site over time. Other projects begin with hundreds or thousands of articles that need to be organized and made searchable.

Have a publication date coming up?

If you’re preparing for a book launch, I can begin with a simple page containing the cover, description, purchase links, and author information.

We can add the rest of the website afterward.

A small launch page can often be completed within about 48 hours once I have the material.

Help with the writing, too

I’m an editor as well as a web designer, so I can help with biographies, book descriptions, introductions, and the other text that needs to go on the site.

You don’t have to prepare perfectly finished copy before getting in touch.
