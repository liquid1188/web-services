What an author site needs

  • A book page for each title with cover, description, endorsements, and buy links to the publisher, Amazon, and your local Catholic bookstore.
  • A press kit page with short and long bios, downloadable headshots, sample interview questions, and a media inquiry form. This is the page that gets you booked.
  • Your writing in one place. Articles at the Register, Where Peter Is, Catholic Review, or your own blog, collected and searchable. Substack essays can be pulled in automatically.
  • Speaking page with topics, past venues, and a booking form.
  • Newsletter signup connected to Substack, Mailchimp, or whatever you already use.

Timing it to a launch

If you have a publication date, tell me on the call. A Starter Site can be live in about 48 hours for a pre-order page, and the full site follows. Roxane Salonen's complete site, with twenty years of columns, went up over a single weekend.

Why not a template

Author templates all look like author templates. A site designed around your book, its cover, its typography, and its subject reads as the work of a serious writer, and that is what a publisher or a conference organizer is judging in the first five seconds.

Who this is for
Authors with a book coming out or already in print, columnists and essayists with a scattered archive, speakers who need a press kit, and writers moving off Wix, Squarespace, or an old blog.
