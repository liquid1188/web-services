What I do

Websites are the core. The rest is the technology around them.

Websites
Custom-coded websites and redesigns
Fast, accessible sites built from scratch, with a simple editor so your staff can update text, photos, events, and posts without calling anyone.
Platform moves
Leaving Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress
Content, archives, and domains moved to a site you own, with old links redirected so search rankings carry over.
Audits
Website and technology audits
A plain-English review of your site, domain, email, and hosting. What is slowing you down, what is costing money you don’t need to spend, and what to fix first.
Setup

Domain and DNS setup, email routing, forms, and secure hosting. The quiet infrastructure most small organizations never get around to fixing.
AI adoption
Practical AI for small teams

Communications
Content, editing, and press materials
Website copy, press kits, bios, and editorial work, from someone who has edited a dozen books and published over sixty articles.
