Ready to move your website?

If your current website has become difficult to update, expensive to maintain, or no longer suits what you’re doing, I can help you move it.

I’ve rebuilt sites from Wix and Squarespace and migrated large archives from older blogging platforms.

I can bring over existing text, photographs, articles, and other material while redesigning the site.

Should you move?

Not necessarily.

Wix, Squarespace, and WordPress can be perfectly good choices, especially for people who want to manage a straightforward website themselves.

But if you’re paying for features you don’t use, finding the editor difficult to work with, or trying to organize a large amount of material, it may be worth considering another approach.

I can review your current site and tell you whether I think rebuilding it makes sense.

What happens to your existing material?

Pages and photographs

I’ll review the existing website and bring over the material you want to keep.

Blogs and archives

Posts and other material can often be transferred programmatically, which is especially useful for larger archives.

Domains and email

I’ll review the domain and email setup before launch and plan the switch to minimize disruption.

Search visibility

I’ll preserve existing page addresses where possible and set up redirects when pages move.

These measures help reduce unnecessary disruption, although search rankings can fluctuate after a migration.

Common questions

Will I lose my Google rankings?

A migration can affect search rankings, even when it’s handled carefully.

I preserve existing page addresses where possible, set up appropriate redirects, and check the site after launch.

These steps help reduce avoidable disruption, although no one can guarantee that rankings will remain unchanged.

What happens to my domain and email?

Your domain remains yours.

I’ll review the existing domain and email records before making changes and plan the transfer to minimize the risk of interruption.

Can you move a large blog?

Yes. I’ve handled archives containing thousands of posts.

I’ll review the current platform and content before giving you an estimate.

How long does a migration take?

A straightforward migration may take about a week. Larger archives and more complex websites take longer.

I’ll provide a timeline with the estimate.

Tell me what you’re working on and what you need. I’ll review the details and give you an estimate.
