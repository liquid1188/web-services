Websites for parishes and Catholic organizations

A parish website needs to make the essentials easy to find.

Mass and confession times, bulletins, office information, sacramental preparation, events, and online giving should all be accessible without having to search through several menus.

I also build websites for apostolates, Catholic nonprofits, and research organizations that need space for publications, programs, events, or fundraising.

What I can include

  • Mass, confession, and adoration schedules
  • Clergy and staff information
  • Ministries and programs
  • Bulletins and homily archives
  • Giving and registration links
  • Publications and newsletters
  • Forms and contact information
  • An editor for routine updates

A website your staff can maintain

Parish offices are busy, and the person updating the website may change from one year to the next.

I provide an editor for routine changes and explain how to use it before the site launches.

I can also handle updates when the parish would rather have me do them.

For institutes and apostolates

Organizations with a larger body of work may need a publication archive, conference registration, a journal, or separate pages for their programs and initiatives.

I’ve built and maintained a nonprofit website of this kind through my own work with the Likoudis Legacy Foundation.

The structure can be adapted to the organization’s needs rather than requiring every ministry to fit into the same standard parish layout.
