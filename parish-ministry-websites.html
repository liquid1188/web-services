What a parish or ministry site needs

  • Mass, confession, and adoration times on the homepage, editable without a developer.
  • Programs and ministries each with a contact and a signup.
  • Bulletin and homily archive as PDFs or posts, searchable.
  • Online giving connected to whatever you already use, or set up if you do not.
  • Staff and clergy page, events calendar, and a sacraments page that answers the questions the office gets by phone.

Ownership and cost

Hosting is free on GitHub Pages and there are no platform fees. The parish owns the files, and nothing renews annually except the domain. When staff change, the new person gets the editor login and the twenty-minute walkthrough, and nothing is lost.
