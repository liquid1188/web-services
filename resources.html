Resources

A short reading list.
Only the pieces worth your time.

Everything here is free, from a primary source, and something I actually use or cite. Eleven links, not a hundred. If you read only one, read the first.

Why speed and design matter

The research behind the numbers on my homepage. Read the originals, not the summaries.

Nielsen Norman Group
The Need for Speed, 23 Years Later
The clearest short account of what a slow page does to a visitor’s attention, with two decades of studies in one place. Start here.
Portent, 2022
Site Speed Is (Still) Impacting Your Conversion Rate
100 million page views. Sites that load in one second convert up to three times better than sites that take five. The source for the 3× on my homepage.
Google
Mobile Page Speed: New Industry Benchmarks
53% of mobile visitors leave a page that takes longer than three seconds. Most of your readers are on a phone.
Stanford Persuasive Technology Lab
Stanford Guidelines for Web Credibility
Ten guidelines from the research behind “75% judge credibility by design.” Short, and still the best checklist for whether a site looks trustworthy.

How people actually read a page

Why long paragraphs and clever layouts lose people, and what to do instead.

Nielsen Norman Group
How Users Read on the Web
They don’t. They scan. The 1997 study that still explains why headings, short paragraphs, and plain words win.
Nielsen Norman Group
10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design
The ten rules every good site follows whether the designer knows their names or not. Useful for judging your own site honestly.

What an author site needs

One guide, from the person publishing professionals send authors to.

Jane Friedman
How to Build an Author Website: Getting Started Guide
What pages you need, what to leave out, and what your bio and book pages are for. Former publisher of Writer’s Digest. Read it before we talk.

Free tools worth using

The ones I use on every build. No accounts to create except Search Console.

Google
PageSpeed Insights
Paste any address and see how fast it loads on a phone and what is slowing it down. Test your current site, then test one of mine.
Google
Search Console
What people typed into Google to find you, which pages they landed on, and whether Google can read your site at all. Free, and most site owners never open it.
Google Search Central
SEO Starter Guide
Google’s own plain-language explanation of how it finds and ranks pages. Ignore anyone selling you SEO who hasn’t read it.
Google Chrome Labs
Squoosh
Shrink a photo to a fraction of its size with no visible loss, in the browser, nothing uploaded anywhere. Oversized images are the number one reason sites are slow.

Leaving Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress

The one document that matters if you move a site and want to keep your Google rankings.

Google Search Central
Site Moves and Migrations
How to move a site without losing what Google already knows about it. This is the checklist I follow on every migration, and the reason page addresses stay the same after a move.
