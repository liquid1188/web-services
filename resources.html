Resources

A selection of studies, guides, and tools I’ve found useful in designing and maintaining websites.

Some are research reports. Others are practical guides for understanding website performance, accessibility, publishing, and search engines.

Website performance

Portent, 2022

Site Speed Is (Still) Impacting Your Conversion Rate

Portent analyzed more than 100 million page views across 20 websites and found a substantial relationship between loading speed and conversions.

In its B2B sample, pages loading in one second had three times the conversion rate of pages loading in five seconds.

That’s a useful reason to take performance seriously, although it doesn’t mean that speeding up an individual website will automatically triple its sales.

portent.com

Google

Core Web Vitals

Google’s framework for measuring loading performance, responsiveness, and visual stability.

Its recommended thresholds provide useful benchmarks for testing how a website performs for real visitors.

web.dev

Nielsen Norman Group

The Need for Speed, 23 Years Later

A review of research into response times, loading delays, and the way waiting affects the experience of using a website.

nngroup.com

Accessibility and usability

WebAIM, 2026

The WebAIM Million

WebAIM’s annual analysis of one million homepages found automatically detectable accessibility failures on 95.9% of them.

Common problems include insufficient color contrast, missing alternative text, and improperly labeled forms.

Automated testing cannot establish whether an entire website is accessible, but it is useful for identifying common problems.

webaim.org

Stanford

Guidelines for Web Credibility

Stanford’s guidelines are older, but much of the advice remains useful, particularly around clear contact information, professional presentation, and making claims that visitors can verify.

credibility.stanford.edu

Nielsen Norman Group

How Users Read on the Web

A foundational article about the way visitors scan web pages and use headings and other visual cues to find information.

nngroup.com

Nielsen Norman Group

10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design

Practical principles for making interfaces understandable, consistent, and easier to use.

nngroup.com

Local search

Google and Purchased, 2016

How Mobile Search Connects Consumers to Stores

In this 2016 study, 76% of people who searched on a smartphone for something nearby visited a business within a day, and 28% of those searches ended in a purchase.

The study is now a decade old, so the exact figures are best read as a snapshot of that time.

thinkwithgoogle.com
